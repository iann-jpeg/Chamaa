FAQ

Questions, Answered.

Learn how CHAMAA supports organized, transparent chama administration.

What is CHAMAA?

CHAMAA is a platform for organizing Chama members, cycles, contributions and payout schedules in one connected workspace.

Who can use the platform?

Community finance groups and investment clubs can use CHAMAA with organization-specific member accounts and roles.

Can one organization manage multiple cycles?

Yes. The platform supports independent cycles under one organization while keeping their records separate.

Can members join multiple cycles?

Yes. One member account can participate in multiple cycles within an organization.

How do participants select payout numbers?

Available participant positions are managed according to cycle rules and the applicable selection deadline.

Does CHAMAA support M-PESA?

The platform includes a modular payment architecture for M-PESA and simulated provider integrations.

Can members view their contribution history?

Yes. Authorized members can review their records and access relevant receipts and statements.

How are payouts tracked?

Payout positions, schedules, approval states and completion records are maintained in the cycle workspace.

Is CHAMAA accessible on mobile?

Yes. The member portal and landing experience use responsive layouts for phones, tablets and desktop devices.

How do I register my organization?

Create a member account, then use the organization registration or invitation flow available to authorized administrators.
